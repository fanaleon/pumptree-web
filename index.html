// MASSIVE REFORESTATION INITIATIVE IN VACA MUERTA, ARGENTINA

The first green token on Pump.fun, for environmental impact

Vaca Muerta is Argentina's largest field — the world's second-largest unconventional gas reserve and fourth-largest oil reserve — accounting for over 70% of the country's production. That growth now needs mitigation. PumpTree proposes reforesting the area with native trees, planted by the community and verified by satellite. Fees fund the work; every parcel becomes a dynamic NFT that grows with it.

// THE MISSION

It's not just a token with a green logo.
It's a commitment to the generations to come.

PumpTree proposes using a percentage of the fees, in supplies and infrastructure, to reforest the arid steppe of Vaca Muerta, to mitigate emissions from the area, so it can be verified and audited — by anyone, at any time, on-chain and via satellite.

The problem

Oil activity has grown and will keep growing. Fracking, flares, thousands of daily vehicles and very few trees make the air harder to breathe for tens of thousands of people. Today's forecasts aren't good, in a province already in water emergency.

WITH PUMPTREE

The initiative

Promoting mass planting, across the community, companies and the state, of species that absorb large amounts of carbon, using advanced technology and proven wastewater-reuse techniques, to guarantee clean, breathable air in under 5 years.
